SETUP GUIDE

Connect your MT4 / MT5 in 5 calm steps.

1 • Receive your key
Your mentor approves your request and sends a AHE key via WhatsApp.

2 • Download the Self-Host EA
Download the .mq5 (MT5) or .mq4 (MT4) file. Copy it into MQL5/Experts/.

3 • Allow WebRequest URLs
In MetaTrader: Tools → Options → Expert Advisors → Allow WebRequest, add the URL printed inside the EA settings.

4 • Attach the EA to a chart
Drag the EA onto any chart, paste your AHE key into the Inputs tab, enable Auto-Trading.

5 • Open the app + go live
Open ambient-host-ea in your browser, paste the same key, you'll see Server Online.
